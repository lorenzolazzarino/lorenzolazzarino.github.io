  \begin{minipage}{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}
    I am a PhD student in Numerical Mathematics at the University of Oxford, advised by Yuji Nakatsukasa and Hussam Al Daas (STFC--CMG). My research concerns (randomized) numerical (multi)linear algebra, with particular interests in randomized low-rank approximations, a-posteriori error estimates, matrix perturbation theory, and the design of synchrotron experiments.
  \end{minipage}}
